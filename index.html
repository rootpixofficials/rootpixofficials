Have a question? Check out the FAQ

Get answers to common questions about our services, process, pricing, and support. We're here to make your tech journey smooth and transparent.

How long does it take to build a website or mobile app?

It depends on the project scope. A basic website may take 2–3 weeks, while a complex mobile app could take 6–10 weeks. We follow agile methodology to ensure fast and flexible delivery.

Do you offer custom pricing or packages?

Yes, we provide tailored pricing based on your requirements. Whether you're a startup or an enterprise, we offer flexible packages that fit your goals and budget.

What technologies do you use for development?

We use modern technologies like React, Laravel, Node.js, Flutter, and AWS. Our stack is chosen based on the best fit for performance, scalability, and future growth.

Will I get post-launch support and maintenance?

Absolutely. We provide support, updates, and performance monitoring after deployment to ensure everything runs smoothly and securely.

Can you help with digital marketing after the product is live?

Yes! We offer SEO, social media management, Google Ads, and more to help grow your online presence and reach your target audience effectively.

Is my data secure with your cloud-based services?

Yes. We follow best practices for cloud architecture, use secure platforms like AWS and Azure, and ensure encryption, regular backups, and access controls.
